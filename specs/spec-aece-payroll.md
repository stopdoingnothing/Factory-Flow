## Problem Statement

**Title:** South African Payroll System

**Current State:** Inefficient and potentially error-prone manual or semi-automated payroll processes leading to compliance risks and resource drain.

**Desired State:** A fully automated, accurate, and compliant South African payroll system that integrates with accounting and banking, manages employee data, and provides comprehensive reporting.

**Pain Points:**
- Manual calculation of statutory deductions (PAYE, UIF, SDL)
- Difficulty keeping up with legislative changes (SARS tax tables)
- Inaccurate or delayed generation of SARS reports (EMP201, EMP501, IRP5)
- Complex handling of various employee types and tax treatments
- Errors in calculating bonuses, allowances, and fringe benefits
- Compliance risks and potential penalties
- Inefficient leave management
- Lack of automation for HR and Accounts departments

**Business Drivers:**
- Compliance with SARS
- Operational efficiency
- Accuracy
- Financial risk mitigation
- Seamless integration

**Constraints:**
- South African legislation
- Annual SARS tax table updates
- POPIA compliance
- Support for diverse employee types

**Success Criteria:**
- Accurate payroll processing
- Valid SARS reports
- Accurate statutory calculations
- Successful system integrations
- User satisfaction
